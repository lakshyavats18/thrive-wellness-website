Data Sovereignty & Store Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Request

Last Updated & Effective: October 3, 2026

Your Privacy Rights & Data Sovereignty

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), GDPR, and CCPA, you have complete control over your personal data. You may request permanent deletion of your Thrive Wellness account, profile details, and all associated health and biometric telemetry records at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

You can choose between instant in-app self-service deletion or submit a web-based erasure request:

Method 1: Directly Inside the Thrive Mobile App (Instant)

  1. Open the Thrive mobile app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear Icon) in the top right.
  4. Scroll down to the "Danger Zone" section and select "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm deletion. Your authentication credentials, profile information, and all recorded metrics will be immediately queued for permanent expungement.

Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can submit an erasure request directly by emailing our privacy team from the email address registered with your Thrive account:

📧 Send Deletion Request via Email →

Or write directly to: support@thrivewellness.io with subject line "Request Account Deletion".

What Data is Deleted & Retention Policy

Permanently Deleted Upon Confirmation:

  • Account credentials, user ID, email address, and authentication tokens
  • Profile avatar image and biographical preferences
  • All historical 8-pillar biometric records, sleep logs, HRV trends, hydration records, and composite Daily Wellness Scores
  • GPS route recordings and workout session telemetry
  • Haven AI coach conversational history and nutritional photo analysis data

Retention Window: Account deletion requests are processed immediately. All production database records in Supabase and cloud backups are completely and permanently expunged within 30 calendar days.

Active Subscriptions Notice

Please note that deleting your Thrive account does NOT automatically cancel active subscriptions billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid recurring charges, cancel your subscription before or immediately after deleting your account:

  • Apple App Store: Open Settings on your iPhone → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Thrive → Cancel Subscription.
  • Google Play Store: Open Google Play Store on your Android device → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Thrive → Cancel.
