Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Thrive ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy and safeguarding your health and fitness data. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards and practices regarding the collection, use, processing, and protection of information gathered through the Thrive mobile application (iOS and Android) and associated web services.

2. Critical Notice: Background GPS Location Data

GPS & Background Location Tracking Disclosure:
Thrive collects precise real-time geolocation coordinates (latitude, longitude, speed, and altitude) when you explicitly initiate an outdoor activity (such as running, jogging, cycling, or walking). This location data is collected in the foreground and the background (when the app is minimized or your device screen is turned off) to ensure that your workout route, pace, distance, and elevation changes are recorded accurately without interruptions.

Location tracking automatically pauses or terminates when you finish or save your active workout session. You maintain full control over whether your activity route is publicly visible to community peers or maintained strictly private in your personal workout archive.

3. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Name, email address, profile avatar, and biographical bio provided during account registration.
  • Authentication credentials and identifiers managed securely via Supabase Auth.

B. Fitness & Health Metrics

  • Workout logs, activity durations, estimated caloric burn, step counts, average pace, splits, and user-selected training goals.
  • Heart rate, elevation changes, and performance streak records.

C. Social & Community Content

  • Workout posts, user captions, shared photos, comments, likes, and direct community messages exchanged between athletes.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • Device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), unique device tokens for push notifications, and crash telemetry (processed via Sentry) to diagnose stability issues.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-leading infrastructure partners to power Thrive's functionalities under strict data processing agreements:

  • Supabase: Scalable cloud database hosting, real-time sync, and encrypted user authentication.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Push notifications (FCM) to deliver live activity notifications and community alerts.
  • Mapbox GL & OpenStreetMap: Map tiles, route visualization, and spatial rendering.
  • Sentry: Application crash tracking and diagnostic error reporting (strictly non-identifying telemetry).

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and optimize our fitness tracking and wellness tools.
  • To accurately render GPS workout maps, distance analytics, and elevation calculations.
  • To enable social feed interactions, community leaderboards, and direct messaging.
  • To send workout reminders, streak notifications, and critical account security alerts.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical bugs and ensure app performance.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal health, location, or biometric data to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with global privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and Apple/Google store requirements), you have the right to:

  • Access, download, or export a copy of your stored workout logs and profile data.
  • Modify or update inaccurate account information directly from your profile settings.
  • Permanently Delete Your Account & All Associated Data: You may trigger account deletion immediately within the Thrive mobile app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or submit a request directly through our web Support Portal. Upon confirmation, all your stored workout logs, routes, and account identifiers will be permanently expunged from our databases within 30 days.

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding your personal data, please contact our Privacy Team at:
