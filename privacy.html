Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Thrive ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy and safeguarding your health and wellness data. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards and practices regarding the collection, processing, and protection of information gathered through the Thrive mobile application (iOS and Android) and associated web services.

2. Health & Biometric Data Principles

Your Health Data Belongs to You:
Thrive collects health and wellness metrics across 8 pillars (Sleep, Stress/HRV, Movement, Hydration, Nutrition, Mind & Energy, Mental Health, and Cycle Phases) solely to compute your personal Daily Wellness Score and deliver Haven AI coaching protocols. We never sell, monetize, or disclose your private biometric telemetry to third-party data brokers or advertisers under any circumstance.

3. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Name, age, gender, email address, and subscription tier selection provided during account registration.
  • Authentication credentials and device security tokens managed via Supabase Cloud Auth.

B. Biometric & Wellness Telemetry

  • Daily logs across sleep duration and sleep quality, HRV (Heart Rate Variability), step counts, active calories, fluid ounces, nutrition tags, focus ratings, and cycle phase information.
  • Wearable sync records from Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin, or Fitbit when explicitly connected by you.

C. AI Interactions & Habit Logging

  • Conversations, queries, and check-ins with Haven AI Coach to generate personalized health protocols.
  • Program progress, milestone completions, and habit streak records.

D. Technical & Security Telemetry

  • Device type, operating system version (iOS/Android), and diagnostic crash telemetry via Sentry to ensure application stability.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure partners to power Thrive's functionalities under strict data processing agreements:

  • Supabase: Scalable cloud database hosting, Row-Level Security (RLS), and encrypted authentication.
  • Firebase / FCM: Push notifications for daily check-in reminders and streak alerts.
  • Sentry: Application crash tracking and diagnostic error reporting (strictly non-identifying telemetry).

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your daily composite health score and identify recovery opportunities.
  • To deliver adaptive, science-backed guidance through the Haven AI Coach.
  • To support structured 21-day wellness programs and habit consistency tracking.
  • To sync biometric inputs from connected smartwatches and fitness wearables.
  • To protect your account against unauthorized access and maintain app security.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, and App Store/Google Play requirements), you have the right to:

  • Access, download, or export a copy of your stored biometric records and profile data.
  • Modify or update inaccurate account information directly from your profile settings.
  • Permanently Delete Your Account & All Associated Data: You may trigger account deletion immediately within the Thrive mobile app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or submit a request directly through our web Support Portal. Upon confirmation, all your stored biometric records and account identifiers will be permanently expunged within 30 days.
