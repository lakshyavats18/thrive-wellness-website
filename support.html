Account & Data Deletion Portal

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store user protection policies, you have complete sovereignty over your account and biometric data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Directly Inside the Thrive Mobile App (Instant)

  1. Open the Thrive mobile app on your iPhone or Android device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear Icon).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and select "Danger Zone" → "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your choice. Your authentication token, profile data, biometric history, and Haven AI chat records will be immediately queued for permanent deletion.

Method 2: Web-Based Data Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to have your data erased, send an email to our automated privacy handler with the subject line "Request Account Deletion" from the email address linked to your account.

Submit Deletion Request via Email →

What Data is Deleted:
• Your account credentials, profile avatar, and settings.
• All 8-pillar daily logs, biometric scores, and trend histories.
• Haven AI coaching conversation history and active program progress.
All records are permanently expunged from our Supabase production servers within 30 days of request confirmation.
