Account & Data Deletion Portal

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store user protection policies, you have complete sovereignty over your account and data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Directly Inside the Thrive Mobile App (Instant)

  1. Open the Thrive mobile app on your iPhone or Android device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear Icon).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your choice. Your authentication token, profile data, workout archives, GPS routes, and community posts will be immediately queued for permanent deletion.

Method 2: Web-Based Data Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to have your data erased, send an email to our automated privacy handler with the subject line "Request Account Deletion" from the email address linked to your account.

Submit Deletion Request via Email →

What Data is Deleted:
• Your account profile credentials, avatar, and biographical bio.
• All GPS route coordinates, split times, and workout metrics.
• Community workout posts, likes, comments, and direct message records.
All data is permanently purged from our Supabase production databases within 30 days of request confirmation.

Troubleshooting & Quick Help

Ensure battery optimization for "Thrive" is set to "Unrestricted" in your phone's Android Settings → Apps → Thrive → Battery. This prevents aggressive OS battery savers from killing the background GPS foreground service.

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Thrive → Select "Always" with "Precise Location" toggled ON.

Simply log in using your registered email credentials on your new device. All your workout records, milestone badges, and history will sync automatically from Supabase cloud storage.
