Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated & Effective: August 28, 2026

100% Risk-Free

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to experience genuine, measurable improvement in your vitality, sleep, and recovery with Thrive. If you are not completely satisfied with your Thrive Starter, Premium, or Elite membership for any reason, you are eligible for a 100% full refund within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase or subscription upgrade.

1. Overview & Scope

This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to all paid subscriptions, plans, and digital services provided by Thrive Wellness ("Thrive", "we", "our", or "us"). Whether you subscribed via the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or directly via our website, we ensure a transparent, fair, and prompt refund experience.

2. The 14-Day Guarantee Terms

  • Applicable Tiers: The guarantee covers all initial subscription purchases for Thrive Starter ($4.99/mo or $29.99/yr), Thrive Premium ($9.99/mo or $59.99/yr), and Thrive Elite ($19.99/mo or $119.99/yr).
  • 14 Calendar Days: Your refund request must be submitted within 14 days of the original purchase or plan upgrade transaction timestamp.
  • No Questions Asked: While we appreciate constructive feedback to help make Haven AI and our 8-pillar tracking better, your refund will be honored without hassle.

3. Subscription Cancellation Rules

You have full autonomy to cancel your recurring subscription at any moment without penalty or early termination fees:

What Happens When You Cancel:
• Retain Active Access: You will continue to have full access to all your paid tier features (unlimited Haven AI coach messaging, 8-pillar biometrics, guided programs, and wearable sync) until the end of your current paid billing period.
• No Future Charges: Your subscription will not renew at the next billing cycle.
• Preserve Your Data: Upon billing cycle completion, your account smoothly transitions to the Thrive Free tier. Your historical scores, daily streaks, biometric archives, and Haven chat memories remain safely intact.

4. How to Cancel & Request a Refund by Platform

Depending on where you purchased your Thrive membership, please follow the platform-specific instructions below:

🍏

Apple App Store

iOS & iPadOS
  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your Apple ID / Name at top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions & select Thrive.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.
  5. To request a refund from Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose "Request a refund", and select Thrive.
🤖

Google Play Store

Android Devices
  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon at top-right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Select Thrive and tap Cancel subscription.
  5. To request a refund within 14 days, contact our team directly at support@thrivewellness.io with your Google GPA order number.
💳

Web & Direct Billing

Website Checkout
  1. Log in to your account settings or open the Thrive App.
  2. Navigate to Profile → Settings → Subscription.
  3. Click Manage / Cancel Plan.
  4. To claim your 14-day refund, simply email our billing desk at support@thrivewellness.io for instant manual processing.

7. Exceptions & Policy Nuances

  • Past 14-Day Window: Refund requests submitted beyond 14 calendar days from the initial purchase date are generally non-refundable. However, you may cancel at any time to prevent all upcoming subscription cycles.
  • Free Trial Cancellations: If you cancel during a free trial period prior to the renewal date, you will not be billed, and no refund is required.
  • Account Violations: Accounts suspended or terminated due to gross violations of our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines forfeit refund eligibility.
